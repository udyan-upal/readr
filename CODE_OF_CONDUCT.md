# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- No publishing other people's private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/udyan-upal). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks these rules.
